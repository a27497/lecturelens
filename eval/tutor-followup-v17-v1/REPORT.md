## What was repaired, and why v17 is the actual version

The independently authored PG reproductions show v15 accepting a real source
anchor with an invented influenza claim/mastery feedback, and a spelled-out full
solution phrased as a Socratic question. Those violate concrete source-grounded
claim and answer-withholding promises. They do not justify demanding unlimited
semantic judgment from keyword rules.

The opt-in v16 precursor replaced free course assertions with exact source spans,
derivations with pure checked value expressions, feedback with exact learner/source
comparisons, and free Socratic text with model-selected controller-owned hint acts.
It passed63 mechanism tests. **Final source review still found a real bypass:**
AST ignores `#` comments, but the renderer displayed the original expression.
An actual PG run published `2+2 # Every loop cures influenza. = 4`.

V16 is therefore retained and rejected, not silently patched or called ready.
The additive v17 identity checks the **entire displayed expression** against a
positive ASCII value grammar before AST interpretation. Comments/control/prose
suffixes cannot become a second unchecked output channel. Five variants reject
through the real runtime and bounded repair; positive arithmetic, finite sequence,
code-excerpt, feedback and all five hint-act paths still work. No blacklist of
clinical/answer keywords or single-question answer is used.

There is no eval/exec, arbitrary name/call, import, attribute access, comprehension
or I/O. The interpreter checks rational arithmetic/list/tuple/range values, with
explicit syntax, node/depth, numeric, exponent, sequence and rendered-result caps.
It proves a selected expression's value, not relevant premises or execution of
the course's Python/R code. Socratic question/reflection text is controller-owned;
model-provided extra labels/text/feedback/solution blocks cannot render there.
Learners may still open full authoritative course citations, including solutions.

Every displayed character still counts toward1600/500; full canonical IDs and
the four-distinct-ID limit remain. No answer is truncated, mandatory point deleted,
budget increased or format-repair allowance extended. New factories require Mock
mode and exclusive non-public schemas. Defaults and old frozen workers remain.
The narrower publication language may fail completeness/feedback/targeted-hint
requirements; their strict references are not adjusted to excuse it.
